## 1. Executive Summary

The Malaysian DIO sits at the intersection of the **MQA quality system + MQF** and the **MOC professional regime under Optical Act 1991 (Act 469)**.

**Currently applicable core instruments (2 Sep 2026) [VERIFIED]:**

- **MQF Second Edition (2017) as updated by MQF Second Edition (2024)** + `Pekeliling MQA Bil.5/2024` — defines Level 4 descriptors, 5 Clusters, 1 credit = 40 notional hours, and new emphases VBE/FLP/GSA/Harmonisation. 2-year transition to 1 Aug 2026.
- **COPPA Second Edition (2017, updated Nov 2018)** + **Rubrik Penilaian 3C (Pekeliling MQA Bil.4/2024)** — 7 evaluation areas, PEO-PLO-CLO constructive alignment, evaluation rubric for accreditation.
- **Programme Standards: Medical and Health Sciences (PS:MHS) Second Edition 2016 (re-issued July 2019 PDF)** — generic health diplomas: 90–105 credits, ≥1,000 hrs practical/clinical/industrial. **No opticianry annex** — discipline-specific requirements are supplied by MOC.
- **Standards for Undergraduate Programme (First Edition 2025, Pekeliling MQA Bil.5/2025)** — general L3-L6 requirements aligned to MQF 2024 (VBE/ESD). DIO programmes without own PS may be cross-referenced; does not replace PS:MHS.
- **Guidelines to Good Practices (GGP): Assessment of Student Learning (AoSL) Second Edition Nov 2023** + **GGP: Programme Development & Delivery (PDD) 2023** — both enforced 30 Nov 2023 (`Surat Makluman Bil.9/2023` / `Bil.8/2023`). GGPs for Industrial Verification of Curriculum (GIVC 2024), Values-Based Education (GVBE Jan 2026), Academic Bank of Credit (ABC Jun 2026) now also current.
- **Optical Act 1991 (Act 469)** + **Optical Regulations 1994** as amended by **P.U.(A) 212/2025 (14 Jul 2025)** — primary legal framework. 2025 amendment is procedural (elections, fees) not scope.
- **MOC Code of Practice for Programme Accreditation — Undergraduate Optometry Degree/Opticianry Diploma Version 2.1 (Jan 2025, 225 pp, supersedes V2 2022)** — *the* discipline-specific mandatory standard for DIO (credits, structure, staff, facilities, clinical training). DIO Section pp.45-64.
- **MOC Lampiran A — Student Entry Requirement (New) Sep 2025 (amendment to Code V2.1)** — current DIO admission thresholds (3Cs) — image-only PDF, verbatim OCR captured.
- **MOC Recognised Qualifications List (Juruoptik)** — only Malaysian DIO currently recognised: Vision College DIO (P.U.(A) 29/2019). No new DIO added 2019-2026.

**Programme-level implications for DIO [VERIFIED]:**

- **MQF Level 4 Diploma; ≥100 credits (MOC floor, overrides MQF 90-min); 3 academic years, ≤43 weeks/yr; ≥500 hrs on real patients under supervision (MOC) within ≥1,000 hrs total practical/clinical/industrial (PS:MHS).** Typical MQR entry Vision College: 100 credits.
- **Dual accreditation mandatory:** MQA (MQF/COPPA/PS:MHS) + MOC (Code V2.1 + Lampiran A). MQA accreditation alone ≠ registrability under Act 469 s.18.
- **OBE is spine:** PEO→PLO→CLO→Content→T&L→Assessment→CQI constructive alignment required; Bloom/revised Bloom, Simpson/Dave (psychomotor), Krathwohl (affective) are *good-practice frameworks* per GGP:AoSL, not regulations.
- **IQAF 2025 replaces COPIA 2009** for institutional audits from 2026 (Pekeliling MQA Bil.8/2025, Evaluation Guidelines Apr 2026) — affects institutional QA, not programme credit/hour minima.

> **Confirmation — Core DIO minima unchanged as of 2 Sep 2026 [VERIFIED]:** Despite 2024-2026 refinements (MQF 2024, Rubrik 3C, IQAF 2025, Code V2.1, Lampiran A Sep 2025, P.U.(A) 212/2025, P.U.(A) 440/2025, GWBL/GVBE/ABC), the fundamental DIO minima remain: **MQF Level 4; 1 credit = 40 hrs; ≥100 credits (MOC) with 3-year duration ≤43 weeks/yr; ≥1,000 hrs total practical/clinical/industrial (PS:MHS) including ≥500 hrs real-patient under supervision (MOC Code V2.1); Lampiran A Sep 2025 entry (SPM/O-Level 3Cs Maths + Science/Bio/Phys/Chem + English + listed alternatives) remains current; Vision College (now Vision University College after 12 May 2025 via P.U.(A) 440/2025) remains the only Malaysian DIO on the First Schedule for Juruoptik registration.** 2024-2026 changes refine QA context and work-based-learning/values/credit-bank guidance, not these minima.

## 3. MQA Framework — Overview

MQA is sole QA agency under Act 679, custodian of MQF, implements COPPA, Programme Standards, GGP, IQAF. All programmes must comply with MQF (`No programme will be accredited unless it is in compliance with the Framework` — MQA Act s.8). QA is via programme accreditation (provisional → full → compliance, ≤5-yr cycle) and institutional audit (now IQAF). DIO falls under Academic sector, NEC 0914.

## 9. MOC / MOH Professional Framework — Overview

MOC is statutory body under Act 469, administered by Ministry of Health. JTC is accountable to MOC; secretariat at MOC.

**Current MOC publications hub:** https://hq.moh.gov.my/moc/publications/ (BM: /terbitan/) — all programme-level guidelines listed with direct PDF downloads (verified live 2 Sep 2026).

### 11.1 DIO Section Verbatim Core Requirements (pp.45-64)

**Introduction (p.45):** Programme created to give formal education to all who wish to become optician. All HEPs offering opticianry must abide with standards; read together with COPPA Second Edition.

**1.1 PLO competencies — 11 mandatory (p.46) [VERIFIED]:**
a) Prescribe and dispense glasses for patients aged **8 years and above**;
b) Perform **non-cycloplegic refraction** and prescribe spectacles for patients aged **8 years and above**;
c) Demonstrate good understanding on anatomy, function and optics of the eye;
d) Do basic measurement, dispensing and edging of ophthalmic lens, as well as frame adjustment;
e) Interpret and dispense a prescription using appropriate lenses in accordance with facial and frame measurement;
f) Advise on appropriate spectacle frame types/materials, and lens forms/materials;
g) Assess vision and **fundamental binocular status** in all patients;
h) Recognize conditions/symptoms requiring **referral and/or emergency referral**;
i) Use technologies in examination of the eye;
j) Communicate effectively with patient;
k) Conduct oneself professionally and ethically.

**1.2 Programme Structure (p.47-48) [VERIFIED — DIO-specific overrides]:**
- **Duration:** Scheduled over **3 academic years** of teaching/learning, revision and examination; max academic year **≤43 weeks**; contact hours must align to SLT per course proforma.
- **Credits:** **Minimum graduating credits must not be less than 100 credits**. Core subjects 80-90%, university/faculty required 10-20%.
- **Practical/clinical training:** **Broad-based (internal training, practical posting/attachment, community services, industrial training) and must be equivalent to at least 500 hours on real patients under supervision** of appointed practical supervisors or local preceptors.

> **Harmonized credit/hour position:** Satisfy **both**: **≥100 credits (MOC)** *and* **≥1,000 hrs total practical/clinical/industrial (PS:MHS)**. At least **500 of those 1,000 must be real-patient clinical** under supervision. MQF 90-min is floor only.

**1.3 Programme Delivery:** Substantial proportion involves practical applications in clinical settings; mechanisms for stakeholder feedback; external examiners in professional examinations every final academic session.

**2.1-2.3 Assessment:** Variety (written/practical finals, continuous assessment, seminars, assignments, **log book**, presentations, practical training with placement report); validity/reliability review; external examiners; appeal policies.

**3.1 Student Selection:** Policy consistent with MOHE; transparent; **disqualified if:** offence against the person, recent/serious dishonesty, serious physical/mental illness, serious communicable disease(s); enrolment commensurate with capacity (increase requires authority approval); **all applicants must have at least credit in English** to complete course (reinforced by Lampiran A 3C English).

**3.2 Articulation & Transfer (p.51-52) [VERIFIED]:** Transfer **only** if (a) currently enrolled, (b) between **recognized HEPs listed in First Schedule**, (c) fulfils **MOC minimum entry**, (d) **MAX 30% credit exemption**, (e) **≥1 year in graduating institution**, (f) **notified to MOC upon transfer**. Certificate in Ophthalmic Technology → max 30% exemption at HEP discretion.

**4.1 Academic Staff (p.54-55) [VERIFIED]:**
- Core clinical teaching: **bachelor degree + 2 years professional experience + MOC-registered** (if degree recognized) OR **bachelor + 5 years + passed Professional Qualifying Assessment + MOC-registered** (if degree not recognized).
- Workforce: **≥60% full-time (1 FT = 3 PT)**, at least bachelor in related field, **≥20% with ≥5 years teaching experience**, **≥60% with optometry/vision science qualifications**, **≥50% Malaysian citizens**.
- **Supervision:** bachelor in optometry + **3 years** + MOC-registered.
- **Ratios:** **lecturer:students 1:20 (whole programme)**, **practical/lab instructors:students 1:10**, **clinical supervisor:students 1:4**.

**4.3 Support Staff (p.56):** Minimum **Resident optometrist/optician – 1, Lab assistant – 1, Registration Clerk – 1**.

**5.1 Physical Facilities (p.57-59) [VERIFIED]:**
- Dispensing lab + optometry skill lab; **refraction cubicles:students 1:4**; edger/focimeter with preventive maintenance.
- **In-house training centre / Private Optometry Practice** providing **General Optometry + Optical Dispensary**.
- **Placement:** Must be included; **each placement ≥4 weeks**; **students:supervisor 1:2**. Placement site criteria: A) practitioner (Name, **MOC reg + current APC**, address, **>3 years experience**, CV, **CPD record, letter of good standing**), B) services (Primary eye care + Optical Dispensary), C) **workload ≥10 patients/week over past 3 months**, D) valid business registration.

**6.2 Programme Leadership (p.60):** Head of opticianry programme must be **registered optometrist in Malaysia, full-time, bachelor in Optometry + 5 years as academician in optometry**, qualified in education/vision care with authority for curriculum.

**7.1 CQI (p.61-62):** Curriculum reviewed **every 4 years** (not 4-5), dedicated QA unit/coordinator, external experts + alumni/employer engagement, student feedback, performance/attrition analysis.

**Section IIIA Database Guide (pp.133-):** MQA-01 (Provisional) / MQA-02 (Full) — provisional submission before programme commencement; full when first cohort enters final year; renewal **≥6 months before expiry**. Database Parts A (HEP), B (Programme description), C (Programme Standards mapping). Full accreditation max **5 years** initial, **5 years** renewal. Every curriculum change must be notified to MQA/JTC.

## 15. OBE: PEO → PLO → CLO → Content → T&L → Assessment → CQI

**MQF→COPPA→GGP chain [VERIFIED]:**

1. **MQF + PS:MHS** — L4 descriptors + 5 Clusters + generic PLOs → institutional PEOs (3-5, 3-5 yrs post-grad) mapped to mission + professional expectations
2. **PEO→PLO** — PLOs at graduation, covering 5 clusters + MOC 11 competencies; PEO-PLO matrix
3. **PLO→CLO** — CLOs per course, mapped to PLOs + clusters; verb choice per Bloom/Simpson/Krathwohl (GGP)
4. **Content** — Syllabus selected to achieve CLOs; sequence ensures progression
5. **T&L** — Methods aligned to CLO domain/level (lectures, labs, simulations, OSCE/OSPE, PBL, WBL)
6. **Assessment** — Tasks/rubrics aligned to CLO level/domain; direct for cognitive/psychomotor, careful for affective; formative + summative; mapping documented
7. **CQI** — PLO/CLO attainment data, PQA pass, employer tracer, clinical logs analysed; curriculum/T&L/assessment adjusted; PEO reviewed with stakeholders (incl. MOC, industry). Mechanism: internal QA unit + monitoring committee + external experts, presented to HEP.

**PS:MHS diploma aims** (knowledgeable, safe, competent; community contribution; professional attitudes; teamwork/communication; technical/problem-solving/ICT/entrepreneurial; lifelong learning) should frame PEOs.

## 16. Bloom / Psychomotor / Affective Domains — Appropriate Use

**Regulatory vs conceptual [VERIFIED]:** Bloom (original/revised Anderson & Krathwohl), Simpson/Dave, Krathwohl/Bloom/Masia are **frameworks** cited in GGP:AoSL Annexes — **good practice, not regulation**. Do not misrepresent as Act/regulation.

- **Cognitive (revised):** remember, understand, apply, analyse, evaluate, create. 2-D table: factual/conceptual/procedural/metacognitive × cognitive process.
- **Psychomotor (Simpson):** perception, set, guided response, mechanism, complex overt response, adaptation, origination. Verbs: measure, align, adjust, assemble, fabricate, calibrate, demonstrate, perform.
- **Affective (Krathwohl):** receiving, responding, valuing, organising, characterising. Verbs: demonstrate commitment, comply, appreciate, value, advocate, internalise.

**GGP expectations for DIO:**
- Each CLO primary domain + level + observable verb (avoid vague “understand” without behaviour).
- Do not assign high-level verb (evaluate/create) with only MCQ assessment.
- Do not overload one CLO with multiple domains without clear assessment.
